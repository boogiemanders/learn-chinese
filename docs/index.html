Help

Add the widget: long-press your Lock Screen, tap Customize, then Lock Screen, tap the widget area and choose Hear to Read. On the Home Screen, long-press, tap Edit, then Add Widget.

Better voices: Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices, then download an Enhanced Chinese (Mainland) and Cantonese (Hong Kong) voice.

Simplified or traditional: switch in the app's Settings tab.
