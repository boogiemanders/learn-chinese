Privacy

Hear to Read: Chinese for ABCs · effective November 2026

Hear to Read does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What stays on your iPhone

Your progress (which words you have reviewed and when) and your settings are saved only on your device, in storage shared between the app and its widgets. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

What we never do

No account. No sign-in. No analytics. No advertising. No tracking across apps or websites. The app makes no network requests.

Speech

Words are spoken with Apple's on-device voices. Nothing you hear or do is sent anywhere.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/boogiemanders/learn-chinese/issues.
